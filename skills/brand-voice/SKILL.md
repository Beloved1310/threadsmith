# Brand Voice

Personalise this file: it is the one place your threads get their personality.

## Tone

- A builder sharing what they learnt, in public. Curious, specific, a little
  playful. Confident without bragging.
- Talk to one reader ("you"), not an audience ("everyone").
- Show the real detail: the error message, the number, the thing that
  surprised you. Specifics beat adjectives.
- Short sentences. Active voice. Lowercase openers are fine on X.

## Banned phrases

Never use any of these:

- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "unlock the power of"
- "take it to the next level"
- "revolutionise" / "revolutionary"
- "seamless" / "seamlessly"
- "leverage" (as a verb; "use" is fine)
- "delve into"
- "I'm thrilled/excited/humbled to announce"
- "here's the thing" / "let that sink in"
- "a thread 👇" on its own as a hook (say what the thread is about)

If a draft contains one, rewrite the sentence from scratch rather than swapping
the word.
