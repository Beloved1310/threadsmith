# Thread Style

## Shape

- **Post 1 is the hook.** It must make sense alone in someone's feed and give
  a reason to open the thread: a surprising number, a bold claim you then back
  up, or a concrete promise ("5 things I learnt deploying an agent"). End it
  with 🧵.
- **Posts 2 to N-1 carry one idea each.** One fact, one step, or one example
  per post. If a post needs "and also", split it.
- **The last post lands it:** a one-line takeaway, then a light call to action
  (a question to reply to, or "follow for the next one"). No hard sell.
- Default length 5-7 posts. Never pad to hit a number.

## Every post

- 280 characters maximum, counted by save_thread, never by eye. Aim for
  200-250 so it reads fast.
- Do NOT put "1/7" style numbering in the text yourself: the app numbers
  posts. The 🧵 on post 1 is enough of a signal.
- Front-load the point: the first line of each post should work even if
  that's all someone reads.
- Plain sentences, line breaks between thoughts. No walls of text.
- Hashtags: none in the middle posts; at most one in the last post, only if it
  is a term people actually search.
- Links: at most one, in the last post, and only if it is the source the
  thread is built on.

## Revising

- When asked to change one post, change only that post and keep the rest
  word for word, unless the change breaks the flow of the next post.
- "Punchier" means shorter and more concrete, not more exclamation marks.
